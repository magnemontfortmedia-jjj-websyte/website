Our Heritage

A Legacy Woven
in Tradition

Born from a reverence for the old world, Magne Montfort draws its essence from centuries of European sartorial mastery. Every thread carries the weight of heritage, every stitch a quiet declaration of excellence.

Our atelier marries time-honoured techniques with a distinctly modern sensibility — creating garments that are not merely worn, but inhabited.

Artisan Excellence

The Art of
the Cable Knit

Each Magne Montfort garment is an act of devotion. Our master knitters employ techniques passed down through generations, working with the world's finest Merino and Cashmere yarns sourced from the highlands of Scotland and the plains of Mongolia.

The signature cable pattern — inspired by mediaeval heraldic motifs — is unique to our house, impossible to replicate by machine. It is our quiet signature, recognisable to those who know.
